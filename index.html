Questions, answered

How do I install the app?

Download the APK, open it from your notifications or the Downloads folder and tap Install. If Android asks, allow installing from this source once.

Will I lose my data when I update?

No. Install the new version over the old one and your account and data remain as they were.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Choose the free plan to get a 7-day free trial. After that, you can pick a plan that fits your practice.

The install is blocked. What should I do?

Open Settings, search for “Install unknown apps”, allow it for your browser, and tap the downloaded file again.
